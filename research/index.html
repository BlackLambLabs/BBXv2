FIELD NOTE / OBSERVATION BEFORE OUTCOME

When did you know it?

A rule is only testable if its inputs were available when the decision was made. Keep the observation, decision and later outcome separate.

Invented values for teaching—not trades or a market forecast
Stage Example record What it establishes
Observation 09:00 · score = 7 The input available at the time
Rule Flag if score ≥ 6 A rule specified before the outcome
Decision 09:01 · flag = yes The rule's output, not a buy recommendation
Outcome 16:00 · measure separately Later evidence; never an earlier input

Try it on paper

  1. Write an observation and its timestamp.
  2. Specify the rule before looking at what happened next.
  3. Record the outcome separately, including missing or uncertain data.

One example cannot establish accuracy, profitability or a working product. Validation needs dated source records, appropriate comparisons and explicit limitations.
